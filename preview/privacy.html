Privacy Policy

DRAFT, not legal advice. This policy is a working draft and must be reviewed by a lawyer before ShoBlax collects any real member data.

What we collect

  • Name, mobile phone and email you enter at sign-up.
  • Sign-in code: stored only as a one-way hash; we can't read it.
  • Device token: a random code saved in your browser so this device stays signed in. We store only a hash of it.
  • Text-consent record: whether you agreed to update texts, the date/time, and the exact wording you agreed to; if you opt out we record that it was withdrawn.
  • Email-updates choice and when you made it.
  • Crew Room posts and reports: thread titles, messages, times, and any reports you file. Other members see your first name and last initial.
  • Basic activity logs (sign-ups, sign-ins, failed code attempts) for security.

How we use it

  • To run your membership, unlock the library and the Crew Room, and help you sign in.
  • To email your sign-in code (we never text codes).
  • To send update texts or emails only if you opted in.
  • To keep the Crew Room safe (rate limits, reports, moderation).

We don't sell your data

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share your phone number or text-consent with anyone for their own marketing.

Where it's stored

Member data is kept in a Google Sheet run by a Google Apps Script owned by ShoBlax, on Google's servers. Emails are sent from that Google account.

Texting

Update texts are optional. If texting is turned on, messages are sent through Twilio, which processes your number to deliver them. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out anytime, START to opt back in, HELP for help. Consent is never required to join.

Book links

Some book links go to Bookshop.org. ShoBlax may earn a commission from purchases made through these links. Bookshop.org's own privacy policy applies on their site.

How long we keep it

We keep member data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account we remove your name, phone, email and tokens within 30 days; we may keep a minimal opt-out record (to honor STOP) and security logs for up to 1 year. Crew Room posts may be removed or anonymized.

Your choices

  • Opt out of texts: reply STOP.
  • Stop emails: contact us (below).
  • Delete your account or see your data: contact ShoBlax at [contact email placeholder].
  • Sign out this device: clear site data in your browser.

Children

ShoBlax is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child signed up, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

We'll post any changes here with a new effective date.
